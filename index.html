FAQ

What equipment do we need?

None. A phone livestream works. We can work with whatever you already use.

Which platforms?

YouTube, Facebook, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts. By default we deliver the finished files so your church can post on your own accounts. We can post for you as an optional add-on if you prefer.

Who approves posts?

You do. By default we deliver finished videos and shorts so you post them on your church’s accounts. If you want us to post for you (optional add-on), nothing goes live until you review and approve it.

How fast is turnaround?

Usually within a few days after Sunday. We aim to have everything ready for the following week.

FAQ

What do I need to send?

Photos and short videos from your phone are enough, or a quick walkthrough of your business. We make the website, posts and reels from that.

Which platforms?

A one-page website plus Facebook posts, and reels on the Growth and Full plans. If you also use Instagram or other accounts, we can talk about that.

Can I review posts first?

Yes. Tell us if you want to approve posts before they go up. We can also adjust anything after it's posted.

How soon can we start?

We can usually get a simple site and the first posts going within about a week. Your first month is free.
